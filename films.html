How they're made

From the live page to the finished film.

Capture the real thing

Headless Chrome walks the live page, click by click, and photographs every state. Analytics and ad tags are blocked and the cookie banner is answered no, so test visits never reach a client's figures.

Compose in code

Each film is an HTML page: the captures placed on a stage, with GSAP timelines for every move, every caption and every label, then rendered frame by frame with HyperFrames at 30 frames a second.

Check every frame

A checker reads the composition and fails text that is too small to read on a phone, text that runs off the frame, and a headline that runs to a third line.

Prove the render

The finished MP4 is compared frame by frame with the browser's own frames on structural similarity, so the film that was checked is the film that was rendered. Then every film is looked at, frame by frame, before it is handed over.

Two films are not here. The SmartCare Living film for the AI Awards 2026 is waiting for the founder to record his parts, and a prototype of an early film duplicates one that is.
