How I work

I find the business problem, decide what to build, and test it against the real thing before it goes live: real bookings, real payments, real emails.

I build with AI coding tools (Claude Code). I set the requirements, review the work, and run the checks and the launch. Each site has automated checks for broken pages, forms, accessibility and claims that the business cannot back up.

Google Ads and analytics. I run Google Ads accounts for two businesses, including conversion tracking, consent mode and offline conversions from the CRM.
