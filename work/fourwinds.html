Case study · My own company

Built, connected and run: FourWinds Digital.

FourWinds Digital is my company. It secures, connects and runs the systems that Irish businesses with 5 to 50 staff depend on, at a fixed price agreed before the work starts. I built the website, the free checks, the client portal and the jobs that run behind them.

  • My roleFounder
  • StartedApril 2026
  • StackNext.js, TypeScript, Supabase (Postgres), Vercel cron, Puppeteer, axe-core, Claude API, Sentry
  • Livefourwindsdigital.com ↗
fourwindsdigital.com
01

A website that states the price

Three products, each at a fixed price: Secured and proven from €2,950, Build from €1,500, and Run from €450 a month. 90 pages in all, including plain-English guides to the laws small businesses meet.

02

A free compliance check

A business answers a few questions about how it works. While it does, the check reads its website and email from the outside. At the end it says which laws apply, what it found, and the three jobs to start with.

  • Five more free checks on the site: AI Act transparency, an AI Act checklist, DPIA, the Cyber Resilience Act and the Accessibility Act
  • No account needed

Try the check ↗

03

A client portal

One login for everything FourWinds builds and runs for a client, beside their invoices and support.

  • Reporting: their figures, with their history kept
  • Automation: their scheduled jobs, with every run logged
  • Knowledge base: answers from their own documents, with the clause each answer comes from
  • Compliance: the client's compliance record and the evidence behind it
04

The Evidence Scan

It scans a client's website and email the way an outsider would, records what each check observed, and on the next scan shows exactly what changed: which checks now pass, and which now fail.

05

Jobs that run on their own

17 scheduled jobs run on Vercel: evidence scans, credential checks, the reporting sync, sales uploads to Google Ads, guards on the ad accounts, a watch on the salon platform, and the outreach. A job that fails is retried, then opens a ticket for a person. Errors go to Sentry, and an uptime monitor watches the outreach health check.

The outreach engine finds Irish businesses from public map listings, checks their website and email security, writes a short email from what it found, and sends two versions of the wording as an A/B test. It reads the replies and records every step in the CRM.

06

Proposals, and a website AI assistants can use

Each prospect gets their own proposal page, and I'm emailed when they open it, with the sections they read and how far they got.

The website also answers AI assistants directly: an llms.txt, a prices page read from the same catalogue, an OpenAPI description and an MCP server at /api/mcp with three tools: get_prices, check_domain and request_call.

How it connects

The whole system, on one page

The portal keeps its records in Supabase, and 17 scheduled jobs do the work nobody should do by hand: the scans, the outreach, reading the replies and sending sales back to Google Ads.
