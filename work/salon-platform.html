Case study · My own product

Booking software built around how a salon works.

A booking system FourWinds sells to Irish hair and beauty salons. Every salon gets three things: a booking page for its clients, a front desk for reception, and the owner's pages. Stylists see their own week on their phones, and FourWinds runs every salon from one operator console.

  • My roleI designed it and built it
  • StartedSeptember 2026
  • Built forThe iPad at the desk, the owner's laptop and everyone's phone
  • Try itThe sample salon ↗
Diary · sample data
01

The client books from their phone

In the salon's own name and colours, the client picks a service, a stylist and a time, and gets the confirmation by email. The salon's name, colours and emails appear everywhere its clients look; FourWinds' brand never does.

02

The desk runs the day from one diary

Every stylist in a column. While a colour develops, the stylist shows as free, so reception can fit a cut into the gap. Phone bookings, walk-ins, check-in and the till all start from the diary.

  • A waiting list with the times still to fill, and a button to offer a freed slot
  • Gift vouchers and memberships
  • A check-in kiosk for clients arriving
03

The owner sees the money

Takings, commission owed, tips, gift vouchers sold, refunds and clients' money held, for any period, each figure against the period before. Every figure opens into its history: who, what, when, amount and VAT.

  • Performance by stylist, and a payroll file
  • Ad clicks, emails to clients and the client list
04

And a website to go with it

FourWinds builds salon websites too. The first is for Joseph Kramer Hair Studio in Sutton Cross, Dublin 13: services, prices, products, a gallery and a contact form, launching on josephkramer.ie.
