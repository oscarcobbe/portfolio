Case study · Home security installer

The shop, the CRM and the calendar behind Smart Space.

Smart Space installs smart doorbells and security cameras across Dublin and Leinster. Another developer built the first version of its website in March 2026. I took it over in April, and I have built and run everything since: the Stripe checkout, the owner's CRM, bookings on his Google Calendar, a new home-network service, 35 guides, and the Google Ads that bring the work in.

  • My roleChief Operations Officer. I built the systems and run them.
  • SinceApril 2026, and still changing every week
  • StackNext.js, TypeScript, Stripe, Supabase, Resend, Twilio, Google Calendar API, Vercel
  • Livesmart-space.ie ↗
smart-space.ie
01

Pay and book in one checkout

In April 2026 I replaced the site's Shopify checkout with Stripe. Customers choose a doorbell, a camera or a bundle, pay by card and pick their installation slot in the same checkout. The slots come from the owner's own calendar, so what the customer sees is what he has free.

  • Installation for Ring, Eufy, Nest, Tapo and Aosu devices, singly or in bundles
  • Installation-only bookings and free home consultations
  • 35 guides I wrote, for people choosing a system or fixing their Wi-Fi
02

Bookings on the owner's Google Calendar

On 20 October 2026 both of the owner's websites moved from Calendly to a booking engine I built. A booking is an event in his calendar: he moves it by dragging it, and the CRM and the reminders follow, because they read the calendar itself.

  • Two customers can never hold the same slot, across both websites
  • Customers move or cancel from a signed link in their email
  • Tested live on the real calendar: three customers booked one slot at the same moment, one got it, two were told it was taken

The engine is open source: read the code ↗

03

A CRM for the owner

Every enquiry and order in one place: where it came from, what was bought, when the visit is, and whether Google Ads counted the sale. Customer emails wait for the owner's sign-off before they go.

  • Each customer's orders, visit, notes and next steps
  • Payment links sent from the customer's record
  • An enquiry can be marked straight from its email
04

The order flow runs itself

When a customer pays, the rest happens in order without anyone touching it: the visit is booked, the customer record is made, the confirmation email goes out, the sale goes back to Google Ads. The reminder before the visit and the review request after it follow, once the owner has signed those emails off.

05

A new service: home network diagnosis

Launched on 29 September 2026. People with slow Wi-Fi can test it themselves room by room, read plain-English guides, or book a €395 Home Network Assessment, which is measured, trialled for three days, then fixed.

  • A free Wi-Fi speed test, run beside the router and in the problem room, graded green, amber or red
  • A Home Network Help Centre of guides
  • Each assessment's monitoring readings land in a portal inside the CRM
06

Google Ads that count real sales

I run the Google Ads account: the campaigns, conversion tracking with consent mode, and sales uploaded back to Google from the CRM. Each enquiry in the CRM says whether Google counted it, and the bidding learns from real sales.
