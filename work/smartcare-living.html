Case study · Fall detection for older people

The website, bookings and advice behind SmartGuardian.

SmartCare Living sells and installs SmartGuardian, camera-free fall detection that lets older people keep living at home, across Dublin and Leinster. I built and run its website, the setup finder, online booking, the Family Advice Centre, enquiry handling and the ads.

The client is one of four finalists for Best Application of AI to Achieve Social Good at the AI Awards 2026.

  • My roleChief Operations Officer. I built the systems and run them.
  • Built2026
  • StackHTML, CSS, JavaScript, Vercel functions, Stripe, Resend, Twilio, Google Calendar API
  • Livesmartcareliving.ie ↗
smartcareliving.ie
01

A setup finder in six questions

Families answer six quick questions, in about 90 seconds: who they are helping, that person's situation and what worries them. The result recommends the SmartCare Living services that fit.

02

Book a home visit online

Families pick a day and a visit window and leave their details. The visit goes straight into the owner's Google Calendar, through the same booking engine as Smart Space, so a slot taken on one website is gone on the other. Customers book their installation the same way, from a private link.

03

A Family Advice Centre

Plain-English guides for anyone helping an ageing parent stay safe at home. Eight topics, each with a full guide: falls, living alone, hospital discharge, dementia, caring for parents, cost and funding, technology, and changes in behaviour.

  • A behaviour checker points a family to the right guide in under a minute
  • A printable behaviour tracker: two to four weeks of notes on sleep, eating, falls, memory and mood, for the GP
04

Every enquiry reaches a person

An enquiry reaches the team by email at once, with a text message as the fallback, and is logged for follow-up. Google Ads and GA4 run under consent mode, so the visitor's answer on the banner decides what is measured.

Every 15 minutes, 24 automated checks open the pages, call the form and booking endpoints, make sure the private APIs refuse strangers, and test the email, text and leads-sheet connections.
