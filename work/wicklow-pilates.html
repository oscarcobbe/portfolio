Case study · Reformer Pilates studio

A website and ten films for a studio in Ashford.

Wicklow Pilates Studio has taught reformer Pilates in Ashford, Co. Wicklow, since 2004: group classes of eight at most, one-to-ones, clinical work after injury, Equestrian Pilates for riders, and live online classes. I built its website and made a film for each thing people ask the studio about.

  • Built2026
  • StackNext.js, Tailwind, Vercel
  • FilmsTen, 13 October 2026
  • Livewicklowpilatesstudio.ie ↗
wicklowpilatesstudio.ie
01

The timetable, with the spaces left

Every class from Monday to Friday, with its level and how many places are left. Classes run in six-week terms, one-to-one slots are marked through the week, and the Friday classes are taught live online.

02

Every class, explained

Reformer classes, one-to-ones, clinical Pilates and Complete Equestrian Pilates are each explained: who it is for and how it starts. Equestrian Pilates has a page of its own, the prices are on the page, and a short guide starts from the visitor's situation rather than their level.

03

Light and dark, phone first

Every page was built for the phone first, and the site remembers a visitor's choice of light or dark theme.
